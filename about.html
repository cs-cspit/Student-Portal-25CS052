About CHARUSAT

Established as the digital nerve center for Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT). Empowering the university community with digital solutions, academic resources, and accessibility-compliant networks.

NAAC "A+" Grade

Accredited with premium institutional standards representing national academic quality.

Top 3 in Gujarat

Ranked consistently among the state's peak universities by GSIRF evaluations.

125-Acre Campus

State-of-the-art green campus in Changa, housing over 10,000 active students.

Academic Legacy & Infrastructure

CHARUSAT has built a legacy of academic excellence since its inception. The campus features world-class computer centers, NBA-accredited course branches, and high-speed campus networks designed to provide industry-aligned engineering training.

Research & Tech Innovations

CHARUSAT drives high-impact innovations, hosting a dedicated Space Technology Centre and running student projects like the development of functional solar cars and F3 racing models. Extra-curricular coding and research programs are supported by active IEEE, ACM, and CSI chapters.
